**The Problem: Real challenges in signal processing**

Signal processing is a critical component of healthcare diagnostics, but it's not without its challenges. EEG (electroencephalogram) signals, for instance, are susceptible to noise and interference from various sources, making them difficult to interpret accurately. Similarly, audio recordings and biosensor data often contain complex patterns that require sophisticated algorithms to unravel.

Moreover, the sheer volume of medical data generated today is overwhelming even with the most advanced processing techniques. The need for real-time analysis and actionable insights has become imperative in this era of precision medicine.
