*   **Algorithm transparency**: Developers must provide clear explanations of their AI algorithms to demonstrate how they arrive at medical decisions.
*   **Clinical validation**: Medical devices must undergo rigorous clinical trials to prove their safety and efficacy.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: What are the key differences between 510(k) and PMA submissions?**
A: A 510(k) submission is required for medical devices that are substantially equivalent to existing products, while a PMA (Premarket Approval) submission is needed for innovative devices that do not have a predicate.

**Q: How can I ensure the security of patient data in my AI-powered medical device?**

**Q: What are some best practices for developing transparent AI algorithms?**
