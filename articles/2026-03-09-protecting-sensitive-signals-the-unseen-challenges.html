**The Problem**

EEG (electroencephalography) and audio data are particularly vulnerable to tampering due to their sensitive nature. These signals can reveal intimate details about a patient's mental health, cognitive abilities, and even neurological conditions. As such, it's imperative that healthcare providers implement robust measures to protect this data.

But what exactly are the challenges facing EEG and audio data in healthcare?

1.  **Data transmission**: EEG and audio signals are often transmitted wirelessly or via internet connections, making them susceptible to eavesdropping or interception.
2.  **Data storage**: These sensitive signals require secure storage solutions that can safeguard against unauthorized access or breaches.
3.  **Data processing**: AI-powered algorithms must be carefully designed to handle these signals without compromising their integrity.
