**The Problem: Challenges in Enterprise AI**

Large-scale AI deployments often lead to a perfect storm of technical debt, data silos, and regulatory non-compliance. Here are some of the most significant challenges enterprises face:

1. **Scalability**: As organizations grow, their AI systems must scale seamlessly to maintain performance and efficiency.
2. **Interoperability**: Different AI components and tools often fail to integrate smoothly, creating data silos that hinder decision-making.
3. **Explainability**: The increasing use of complex machine learning models raises concerns about interpretability and trustworthiness.
4. **Regulatory Compliance**: Enterprises must adhere to increasingly stringent regulations governing AI development, deployment, and usage.

To address these challenges, organizations need a robust governance framework for their AI systems. This involves implementing processes, policies, and technologies that ensure accountability, transparency, and security across the entire AI lifecycle.
