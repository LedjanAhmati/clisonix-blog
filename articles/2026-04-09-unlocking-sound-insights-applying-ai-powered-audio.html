**The Problem**

Traditional audio processing techniques are often inadequate for real-world healthcare applications due to their limitations in handling noisy, dynamic environments. Moreover, the sheer volume of audio data generated by medical devices such as EEGs, ECGs, and ultrasound machines can overwhelm clinicians, making it difficult to identify critical signals amidst the noise.

Furthermore, current solutions often rely on manual annotation, which is time-consuming and prone to human error. The result is a fragmented landscape where valuable insights remain locked away, waiting for innovative solutions to unlock them.
