**The Problem: Challenges in AI-ML Systems**

Developing effective BCIs is a complex task due to the inherent noise and variability present in EEG signals. Traditional signal processing techniques often fail to accurately capture the subtle patterns and features necessary for reliable control. Additionally, as BCIs become more sophisticated, they require ever-increasing amounts of data and computational resources.

Current AI-ML systems struggle with the following challenges:

* **Data quality**: EEG signals are inherently noisy, making it difficult to extract meaningful information.
* **Computational power**: Processing large datasets in real-time requires significant computational resources.
* **Latency**: Delays between signal acquisition and processing can severely impact BCI performance.
