**The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio biomarkers hold immense potential for revolutionizing healthcare diagnostics. However, extracting actionable insights from raw audio signals is a daunting task due to several reasons:

1.  **High dimensionality**: Audio data often consists of multiple channels (e.g., EEG, ECG), each with its own frequency spectrum and amplitude variations.
2.  **Variability**: Speech patterns, voice characteristics, and ambient noise can introduce significant variability in audio signals, making it difficult to identify reliable biomarkers.
3.  **Complexity**: Traditional signal processing techniques often fail to capture subtle patterns and relationships between different audio features.

To overcome these challenges, our team has developed novel architectures and algorithms that seamlessly integrate with existing medical diagnostic pipelines.
