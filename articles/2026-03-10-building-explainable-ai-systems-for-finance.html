**The Problem**

Developing explainable AI systems in finance is a daunting task due to several reasons:

1. **Complexity**: Financial models often involve intricate relationships between multiple variables, making it challenging to identify the underlying causes of predictions.
2. **Lack of transparency**: Traditional machine learning algorithms, such as neural networks, can be opaque, making it difficult to understand why certain decisions are made.
3. **Risk and uncertainty**: Financial institutions operate in a high-stakes environment where a single mistake can have significant consequences.

To overcome these challenges, we need AI systems that provide transparent, interpretable, and explainable results.
