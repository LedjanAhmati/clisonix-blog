**The Problem**

Regulatory landscapes for BCI medical devices are characterized by fragmented and often unclear guidelines. In the United States, for instance, the FDA's 510(k) clearance process is a crucial step in obtaining market approval. However, navigating this process can be daunting due to the need to address multiple regulatory frameworks, including:

* Medical Device Regulation (MDR)
* In Vitro Diagnostic Medical Devices Regulation (IVDR)
* General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

These regulations demand meticulous documentation, precise classification, and rigorous testing, which can significantly slow down product development.
