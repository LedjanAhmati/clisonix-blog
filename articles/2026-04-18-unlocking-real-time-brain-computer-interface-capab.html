This shift is particularly relevant for brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), which require low-latency and high-fidelity EEG signal processing.

**The Problem: Challenges in Edge Computing**

While edge computing offers numerous benefits, it also presents unique challenges. These include:

* **Real-time processing:** Processing large amounts of data in real-time at the edge requires significant computational resources.
* **Latency:** Ensuring low latency is critical for applications like BCIs, where even minor delays can impact accuracy and usability.
* **Data fusion:** Integrating multiple data sources, such as EEG, audio, and biosensors, can be complex and resource-intensive.
