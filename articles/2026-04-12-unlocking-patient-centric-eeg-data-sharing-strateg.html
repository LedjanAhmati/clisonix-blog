As healthcare providers increasingly adopt AI-powered solutions to analyze and interpret patient data, ensuring the confidentiality of sensitive information has become a pressing concern. The growing need for data-driven insights in neuroscience research and clinical practice has led to an explosion of EEG (electroencephalogram) data generation. However, safeguarding brain signal confidentiality is a daunting task, particularly when sharing these sensitive signals with external parties or across distributed healthcare nodes.

**The Problem**

EEG data contains highly personal and sensitive information about an individual's cognitive state, neurological conditions, and emotional experiences. Mismanagement of EEG data can lead to unauthorized access, data breaches, or even misuse in targeted advertising or malicious activities. Existing solutions often rely on outdated encryption methods or cumbersome manual processes, which are inefficient and error-prone.
