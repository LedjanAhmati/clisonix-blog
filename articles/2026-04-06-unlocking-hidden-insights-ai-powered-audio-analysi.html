**The Problem**

Audio processing is a challenging field, particularly when applied to the realm of healthcare. Traditional methods often struggle to keep pace with the dynamic nature of medical data, leading to issues such as:

* Signal degradation: Audio signals can be prone to noise, distortion, and other forms of degradation that compromise their integrity.
* Limited scalability: Current approaches often rely on manual processing or simple algorithmic techniques that fail to scale with increasing amounts of data.
* Poor interpretability: The results of audio analysis are frequently difficult for non-experts to understand, making it challenging to communicate insights effectively.
