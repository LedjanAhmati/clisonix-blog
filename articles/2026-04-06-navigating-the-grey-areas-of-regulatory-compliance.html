**The Problem**

Regulatory frameworks are struggling to keep pace with the rapid advancements in AI and ML technologies. The FDA's guidelines for software as a medical device (SaMD) are still evolving, and the EU's Medical Device Regulation (MDR) has introduced new requirements for the certification of AI-based medical devices. However, these regulations often lack clear guidance on specific aspects such as data privacy, security, and algorithmic transparency.

Moreover, the increasing complexity of AI-powered medical devices has led to a growing concern about their reliability and performance. With multiple stakeholders involved in the development and deployment process, there is a higher risk of errors, misconfigurations, or unintended consequences. In such scenarios, regulatory compliance becomes even more challenging, as healthcare organizations must ensure that their AI-powered medical devices meet the required standards while maintaining patient safety.
