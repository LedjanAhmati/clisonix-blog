**Technical Deep Dive**

Our Edge AI architecture is designed to handle the unique demands of medical device data processing. The system consists of three primary components:

1. **Data Ingestion**: Our Signal Fabric module collects and preprocesses EEG, audio, and biosensor signals in real-time, providing a unified feed for analysis.
2. **Pattern Recognition**: Our LIAM Binary Algebra performs matrix algebra computations on the preprocessed data, identifying patterns and anomalies that would be difficult to detect manually.
3. **Decision Support**: The results from our pattern recognition module are fed into the Tide Engine, which ensures consistent state across distributed nodes, enabling real-time decision support for medical professionals.
