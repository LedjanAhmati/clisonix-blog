In today's era of precision medicine, healthcare professionals are increasingly relying on advanced technologies to uncover new insights from complex medical signals. Among these, electroencephalography (EEG) signals hold significant promise for diagnosing neurological disorders, monitoring brain function, and understanding cognitive processes. However, extracting meaningful information from EEG signals is a challenging task that requires sophisticated signal processing techniques.

**The Problem: Challenges in Signal Processing**

Existing methods for processing EEG signals often rely on rudimentary approaches that fail to capture the full complexity of the data. Traditional filtering methods can introduce artifacts and distortions, while machine learning-based approaches may struggle with noise, variability, and limited sample sizes. Moreover, most current solutions focus on individual aspects of EEG signal analysis, neglecting the intricate relationships between different frequency bands, signal components, and physiological processes.
