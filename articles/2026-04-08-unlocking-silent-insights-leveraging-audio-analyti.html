**The Problem: Challenges in Audio Processing**

While the potential for EEG analysis is vast, there are several challenges that must be addressed before it becomes a standard practice in healthcare. These include:

* **Signal noise**: EEG signals can be corrupted by external noise sources, such as electrical interference or equipment malfunction.
* **Data complexity**: Analyzing large datasets from EEG sensors requires sophisticated algorithms to filter out irrelevant information and extract meaningful insights.
* **Real-time processing**: EEG data is typically acquired in real-time, requiring efficient processing algorithms that can handle high-speed streams.
