**The Problem**

Ensuring the confidentiality and integrity of patient data is a pressing issue in healthcare AI. With the increasing use of wearable devices, electroencephalography (EEG) sensors, and other biosensors, the amount of data generated is skyrocketing. This has created a perfect storm of challenges for data privacy:

1. **Scalability**: Handling massive volumes of neural signals while maintaining performance and accuracy.
2. **Security**: Protecting sensitive patient information from unauthorized access or breaches.
3. **Regulatory Compliance**: Adhering to stringent regulations, such as HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) in the United States.
