**Technical Deep Dive**

Our team at Clisonix has been working on developing an AI-powered EEG signal processing framework that leverages the strengths of distributed systems and quantum-resistant encryption. We've integrated several key technologies to create a seamless and secure experience:

* **CRDT Merge Layer**: Our CRDT (Conflict-Free Replicated Data Type) merge layer enables multiple clinicians to edit patient data simultaneously, ensuring consistency across nodes in a distributed healthcare network.
* **Post-Quantum Encryption**: We use quantum-resistant encryption to future-proof our system against potential attacks. This ensures that sensitive medical data remains secure and protected.
* **Tide Engine**: Our Tide Engine ensures a consistent state across all nodes in the distributed network, guaranteeing that each update is reflected accurately and promptly.

By combining these technologies, we've created an architecture that can handle large volumes of EEG data while maintaining high accuracy and low latency.
