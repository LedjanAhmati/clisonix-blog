As the European Union's AI Act takes shape, the healthcare industry is bracing for significant changes to its AI development and deployment practices. The Act aims to harmonize regulations across member states, ensuring accountability and transparency in AI decision-making processes. For healthcare organizations like Clisonix, this means re-evaluating existing infrastructure and adapting to new standards.

**The Problem: Regulatory Compliance Challenges**

Healthcare companies must navigate a complex landscape of regulatory requirements, including GDPR, HIPAA, and now the EU AI Act. Ensuring compliance is daunting due to:

1. **Data management**: Multiple data sources, varying formats, and sensitive information make data governance challenging.
2. **Model explainability**: Transparency in AI decision-making processes is crucial for trust and accountability.
3. **Security**: Protecting patient data from unauthorized access or malicious attacks requires robust security measures.
