**Technical Deep Dive: Architecture, Algorithms, Implementation**

Our solution begins with Signal Fabric, an advanced data processing engine that weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to create a comprehensive picture of urban energy. This fabric is then fed into the Tide Engine, a distributed computing platform ensuring consistent state across various nodes.

Within this framework, our Labor Intelligence Engine (LIAM) utilizes Binary Algebra to compute patterns in human labor metrics, such as productivity, efficiency, and quality. These computations inform optimized resource allocation, reducing waste and improving overall city performance.
