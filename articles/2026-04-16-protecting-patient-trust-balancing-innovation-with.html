**The Problem: Real Challenges in Data Privacy**

EEG-based applications are becoming increasingly popular for diagnosing neurological conditions, such as epilepsy or Parkinson's disease. Clisonix's Tide Engine plays a critical role here, ensuring consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes. However, this increased adoption also introduces new vulnerabilities:

*   **Inadequate storage**: Insufficient storage capacity or inefficient data management can compromise the integrity of sensitive medical information.
*   **Lack of transparency**: Patients often struggle to understand how their data is being used, stored, and protected.
