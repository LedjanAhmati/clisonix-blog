**The Problem: Real Challenges in Signal Processing**

EEG signals are inherently complex, with multiple frequency bands, amplitude fluctuations, and non-stationarity. Traditional signal processing techniques often fail to capture the nuances of these signals, leading to incomplete or inaccurate interpretations. Furthermore, the sheer volume of EEG data generated from research studies and clinical applications poses significant challenges for manual analysis.
