**The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio analytics have long been touted as the holy grail of healthcare insights. However, extracting actionable information from these recordings is often hindered by technical challenges:

1. **Signal quality**: Patient recordings are frequently plagued by noise, artifacts, and equipment malfunctions, making it difficult to discern meaningful patterns.
2. **Variability**: Human speech patterns, breathing rates, and other physiological metrics exhibit inherent variability, complicating the analysis of audio data.
3. **Scalability**: As healthcare providers seek to leverage AI for predictive analytics, the sheer volume of patient recordings poses significant scalability challenges.
