**The Problem**

While the benefits of AI are undeniable – improved patient outcomes, reduced administrative burdens, and enhanced operational efficiency – the real-world challenges associated with implementing XAI in enterprise solutions remain significant. These include:

* **Lack of transparency**: Without clear explanations for model decisions, it's impossible to understand where errors may be occurring or identify areas for improvement.
* **Limited interpretability**: Traditional machine learning models often rely on complex mathematical frameworks that obscure the underlying reasoning process.
* **Data quality issues**: Poor data management and inconsistent data formats can compromise the accuracy of AI-driven insights.
