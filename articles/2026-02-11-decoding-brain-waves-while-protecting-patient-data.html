**The Problem**

The exponential growth of brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and neurotechnologies has led to an explosion in data generation, from EEG signals to behavioral patterns. 1. **Patient identifiable disclosure**: Accidental exposure of sensitive information, violating HIPAA guidelines and putting patients at risk.
2. **Lack of transparency**: Unclear or incomplete data processing practices, eroding trust in the healthcare system.

To mitigate these risks, we need to adopt a multi-faceted approach that balances innovation with regulatory compliance. This involves not only implementing robust security measures but also fostering a culture of data stewardship within our organization.
