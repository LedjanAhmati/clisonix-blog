**The Problem: Real Challenges in Edge Computing**

Edge computing has gained significant attention in recent years due to its ability to process data closer to the source, reducing latency and increasing efficiency. However, implementing edge-enabled analytics in healthcare settings comes with unique challenges. Scalability, security, and data integration are just a few of the hurdles that must be overcome.

EEG signal processing, in particular, poses significant computational demands due to the high-frequency nature of brain activity. Traditional approaches often rely on centralized architectures, which can lead to delayed insights, compromised patient safety, and increased costs.
