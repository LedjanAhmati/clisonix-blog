**The Problem: Regulatory Compliance**

Retailers are no strangers to regulatory challenges, but the EU AI Act brings a new level of complexity to the table. With its focus on transparency, explainability, and accountability, the act requires companies to ensure their AI systems are not only accurate but also fair and unbiased. This is particularly challenging for retail, where AI-driven decision-making is increasingly used in areas like pricing, supply chain management, and customer segmentation.

For instance, imagine a retailer using Clisonix's Tide Engine to manage inventory across multiple locations. The system uses real-time data from various sources to optimize stock levels and minimize waste. However, if the algorithm relies on biased data or incorporates discriminatory practices, it may inadvertently perpetuate existing inequalities in pricing or product availability. Compliance with the EU AI Act demands that retailers like yours can justify their AI-driven decisions and demonstrate their systems' transparency.
