The insurance industry is on the brink of a crisis. As artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) systems become increasingly prevalent in underwriting, claims processing, and policy management, concerns about explainability and transparency are growing louder. Insurers must now navigate the complex landscape of AI-driven decision-making while ensuring that their systems are transparent, reliable, and compliant with regulatory requirements.

**The Problem:**

Traditional AI/ML systems often rely on opaque algorithms and black box models, making it challenging to understand how they arrive at a particular decision or prediction. This lack of transparency can erode trust between insurers and their customers, as well as among regulators, who demand clear explanations for complex decisions.

Furthermore, the increasing reliance on data-driven AI/ML systems has created new challenges in terms of data quality, bias, and fairness. As AI models become more sophisticated, they also become more vulnerable to subtle biases and errors that can have significant consequences in high-stakes insurance environments.
