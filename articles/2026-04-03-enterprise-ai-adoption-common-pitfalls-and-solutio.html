**The Problem: Real Challenges in Enterprise AI**

When implementing AI at scale, organizations often face a multitude of issues:

1. **Scalability**: As datasets grow exponentially, traditional architecture can become bottlenecked, leading to performance degradation.
2. **Complexity**: Integrating multiple AI models, algorithms, and frameworks introduces unnecessary complexity, making it challenging to maintain and optimize the system.
3. **Reliability**: Ensuring consistent results across distributed nodes is a daunting task, especially when dealing with sensitive healthcare data.
4. **Security**: Protecting patient information while enabling secure collaboration between stakeholders is a pressing concern.

These challenges necessitate the development of robust AI infrastructure that can handle the demands of enterprise adoption.
