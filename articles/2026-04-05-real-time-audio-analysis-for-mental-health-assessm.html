**The Problem**

Audio signals are inherently noisy and dynamic, making it difficult to extract meaningful insights from them. Traditional signal processing techniques often fail to capture the nuances of human speech or behavior, leading to inaccurate assessments. Moreover, audio analysis requires a deep understanding of acoustics, signal processing, and machine learning – expertise that is not commonly found in healthcare professionals.
