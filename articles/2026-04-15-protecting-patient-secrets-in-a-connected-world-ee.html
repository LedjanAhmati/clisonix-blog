With the increasing adoption of IoT devices, such as EEG headsets and wearable biosensors, sensitive patient information is being transmitted over networks, creating potential entry points for unauthorized access. Additionally, the use of audio analytics in healthcare settings raises concerns about microphone eavesdropping and sensitive conversation interception.

To mitigate these risks, Clisonix's Tide Engine ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, maintaining data integrity and reducing the risk of unauthorized access. However, this is just one aspect of a comprehensive data protection strategy.
