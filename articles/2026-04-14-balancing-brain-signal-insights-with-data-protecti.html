In today's data-driven healthcare landscape, Electroencephalography (EEG) has emerged as a powerful tool for uncovering insights into brain function and behavior. However, the rich dataset generated by EEG devices also raises significant concerns about patient data privacy and security. As we navigate this complex ethical terrain, it is essential to strike a balance between harnessing the potential of EEG for medical breakthroughs while protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.

**The Problem**

EEG-based healthcare applications generate vast amounts of personal data, which can be vulnerable to breaches and exploitation. The consequences are dire: compromised patient confidentiality, reputational damage, and even physical harm. Moreover, regulatory frameworks like GDPR and HIPAA mandate rigorous compliance measures, adding complexity to EEG system design. As a Clisonix solution architect, I have encountered numerous challenges in ensuring data protection while maintaining the accuracy and precision of brain signal analysis.
