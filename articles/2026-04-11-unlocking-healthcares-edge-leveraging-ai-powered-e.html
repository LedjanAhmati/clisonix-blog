The rise of edge computing has revolutionized the way healthcare organizations process and analyze vast amounts of patient data. With the increasing adoption of wearable devices, mobile health apps, and IoT sensors, edge computing enables real-time processing and analysis at the point of care, reducing latency and improving decision-making.

However, traditional edge computing architectures often rely on centralized approaches, which can lead to significant challenges in healthcare settings. The sheer volume of data generated by medical devices, combined with the need for low-latency processing and secure data transmission, poses a daunting task for healthcare IT teams.

**The Problem: Real Challenges in Edge Computing**

In healthcare, edge computing faces unique challenges due to:

1. **Scalability**: Handling massive amounts of data from multiple sources, including EEG, audio, and biosensors.
2. **Security**: Ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive patient data at the device periphery.
3. **Latency**: Achieving real-time processing and analysis without compromising on accuracy or decision-making.
