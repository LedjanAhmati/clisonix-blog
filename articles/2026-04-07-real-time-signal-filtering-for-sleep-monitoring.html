**The Problem**

Signal processing is an essential aspect of sleep monitoring, as it involves filtering out unwanted noise and artifacts from physiological signals such as EEG, audio, and biosensors. Traditional methods often rely on static filtering techniques, which can be inadequate in real-time applications due to changing signal characteristics. Moreover, these approaches might not account for the interdependencies between different sensor streams.
