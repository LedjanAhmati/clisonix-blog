**Technical Deep Dive**

At Clisonix, our team has been working on leveraging BCIs to develop more sustainable technologies. This has led us to integrate our proprietary Signal Fabric with the Tide Engine, a distributed computing architecture designed specifically for healthtech applications.

Signal Fabric enables seamless integration of various data streams from EEG, audio, and biosensors, allowing for richer insights into human behavior and cognition. The Tide Engine ensures consistency across these nodes by implementing sophisticated state management and synchronization protocols.

This approach not only reduces energy consumption but also enhances decision-making accuracy for businesses and organizations.
