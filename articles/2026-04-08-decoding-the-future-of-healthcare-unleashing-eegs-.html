**The Problem**

Current signal processing methods rely on oversimplified approaches that neglect the complexity and intricacies of brain activity patterns. These limitations are exemplified by:

* Time-domain analysis: Focusing solely on amplitude and frequency characteristics fails to capture the intricate relationships between different frequency bands.
* Spectral analysis: Using Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) or similar techniques often results in poor time-frequency resolution, missing subtle changes in brain activity.
* Manual feature extraction: The human analyst's subjective interpretation of EEG signals can lead to inconsistent and unreliable findings.
