**The Problem**

HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) compliance is a top priority for healthcare organizations, yet many struggle to meet the stringent requirements for securing sensitive data. The main issue lies in the complexity of implementing robust security measures while allowing authorized personnel to access critical information. This creates a delicate balance between protecting patient confidentiality and facilitating collaboration among healthcare professionals.

Within the realm of EEG data, the challenge is even more pronounced due to its unique characteristics. EEG signals contain highly personal and sensitive information about an individual's cognitive state and neurological function. The sheer volume of data generated by wearable devices and monitoring systems adds to the complexity of securing this sensitive information.
