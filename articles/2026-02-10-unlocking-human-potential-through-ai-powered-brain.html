**The Problem**

Currently, sustainable tech is hindered by several significant challenges:

1.  **Limited accessibility**: BCIs require complex hardware setups, making them inaccessible to many individuals.
2.  **Data fragmentation**: EEG, audio, and biosensor streams are often isolated, preventing seamless integration and analysis.
3.  **Scalability limitations**: Existing solutions rely on manual processing, hindering large-scale adoption.

These challenges hinder the widespread adoption of BCIs in sustainable health applications.

**Technical Deep Dive**

To address these issues, our team at Clisonix developed two critical technologies:

1.  **Tide Engine**: This ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, guaranteeing seamless integration and scalability.
2.  **Signal Fabric**: Our proprietary Signal Fabric weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams, providing a unified data pipeline for comprehensive analysis.
