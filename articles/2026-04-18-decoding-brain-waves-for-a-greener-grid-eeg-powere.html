Renewable energy sources like solar and wind have made significant strides in recent years. To mitigate this issue, utilities are turning to innovative technologies that can help optimize energy consumption.

One such technology is EEG-powered demand response systems. By leveraging electroencephalography (EEG) signals from brain activity, these systems can predict changes in consumer behavior and adjust energy usage accordingly. But how does it work?
