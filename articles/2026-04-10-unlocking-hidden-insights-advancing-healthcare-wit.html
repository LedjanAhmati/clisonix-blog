Audio signal processing is a vital component of healthcare diagnostics, particularly in areas such as speech therapy, hearing loss, and cardiovascular disease. However, the sheer complexity and variability of audio data pose significant challenges:

* **Variability**: Audio signals are inherently dynamic, influenced by factors like environmental noise, equipment quality, and patient conditions.
* **Complexity**: Signals often consist of multiple frequency components, requiring sophisticated processing techniques to extract meaningful information.
* **Scalability**: As healthcare datasets grow exponentially, traditional audio processing methods struggle to keep pace.
