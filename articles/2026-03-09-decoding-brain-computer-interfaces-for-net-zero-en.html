**The Problem**

Traditional BCIs rely heavily on hardware-based approaches, which can be cumbersome and expensive to implement. Moreover, these systems often require extensive computational resources, leading to increased energy consumption. In contrast, software-centric solutions like those developed by Clisonix offer a more efficient alternative. However, their adoption has been hindered by several challenges:

1. **Signal Noise**: EEG signals are inherently noisy, making it difficult to extract meaningful data.
2. **Data Standardization**: Integrating multiple sensor streams (e.g., EEG, audio, and biosensors) into a coherent framework is a daunting task.
3. **Energy Efficiency**: High-energy consumption remains a significant concern in healthcare settings.

**Technical Deep Dive**

To address these challenges, Clisonix has developed two key technologies: the Tide Engine and Signal Fabric.

*   **Tide Engine**: This distributed computing engine ensures consistent state across various nodes, allowing for seamless data integration and processing.
*   **Signal Fabric**: By weaving together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams, this technology provides a robust framework for signal processing and analysis.
