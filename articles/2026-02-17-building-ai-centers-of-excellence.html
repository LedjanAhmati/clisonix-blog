**The Problem**

Despite the growing demand for AI-driven solutions in healthcare, many organizations struggle to establish effective AI CoEs. Common challenges include:

1. **Scalability**: As datasets grow, traditional architectures often falter under the weight of increased computational demands.
2. **Consistency**: Ensuring consistent state across distributed nodes can be a significant challenge, particularly when dealing with heterogeneous infrastructure.
3. **Latency**: Meeting stringent latency requirements is crucial for real-time decision-making in healthcare.

To overcome these challenges, it's essential to develop AI systems that are scalable, consistent, and low-latent.
