**The Problem: Real Challenges in Signal Processing**

Signal processing is a crucial step in extracting actionable insights from medical data. Unfortunately, traditional methods often fall short when dealing with the intricacies of healthcare signals. For instance:

* EEG signals can be noisy and require advanced filtering techniques
* Audio signals from stethoscopes or ultrasound devices may have varying frequency ranges
* Biosensor streams often need to be synchronized across multiple nodes

Current signal processing frameworks struggle to adapt to these complex requirements, leading to reduced accuracy and efficiency in healthcare applications.
