**The Problem**

Regulatory compliance is a major hurdle for many organizations implementing AI in healthcare. The sheer volume of personal data generated by patients, combined with the complexity of AI systems, makes it difficult to ensure that sensitive information is handled correctly. GDPR requirements demand transparency, accountability, and security measures that are often at odds with the black-box nature of many AI models.
