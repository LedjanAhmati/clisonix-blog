Current audio processing techniques often rely on manual analysis or primitive algorithms, leading to limited understanding and interpretation of the complex patterns embedded in these signals. 1. **Signal noise**: Background chatter, device interference, and other external factors can drown out valuable information.
2. **Data heterogeneity**: Diverse sources, formats, and sampling rates make it difficult to integrate and analyze audio streams.
3. **Pattern recognition**: Identifying coherent patterns within noisy signals is a daunting task for human analysts.
