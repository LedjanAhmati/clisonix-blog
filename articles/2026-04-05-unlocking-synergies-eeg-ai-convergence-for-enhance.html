**Technical Deep Dive**

Our solution leverages three key Clisonix technologies to overcome these challenges:

1. **ALDA Labor Array**: A deterministic task scheduling framework, ALDA ensures efficient resource allocation across compute nodes.
2. **Tide Engine**: By maintaining a consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, Tide Engine facilitates seamless integration and data exchange between systems.
3. **Neural Mesh**: This edge-to-cloud AI inference platform enables sub-millisecond latency while processing EEG signals.

Our architecture consists of three primary components:

1. **Data Ingestion Layer**: Utilizing ALDA Labor Array, we orchestrate the ingestion of EEG data from various sources, including medical devices and EHR systems.
2. **Signal Processing Layer**: The Tide Engine ensures consistent state management across nodes, allowing for efficient processing of EEG signals using AI algorithms.
3. **Decision Support Layer**: Powered by Neural Mesh, our decision support system leverages AI-driven insights to provide real-time recommendations to healthcare professionals.
