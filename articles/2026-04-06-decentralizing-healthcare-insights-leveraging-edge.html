**The Problem**

Edge computing is not without its challenges. Current implementations face scalability issues, high latency, and poor performance when dealing with large datasets. Moreover, as the volume and variety of data grow, so does the complexity of managing these edge nodes. It's like trying to orchestrate a symphony while blindfolded – you need a reliable conductor (in this case, our Tide Engine technology).

Our Signal Fabric solution is designed to handle the vast amounts of EEG, audio, and biosensor streams generated by patients. However, even with Signal Fabric's high-performance capabilities, edge computing's limitations must be addressed.
