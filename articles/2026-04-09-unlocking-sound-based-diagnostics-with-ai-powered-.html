The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing

Audio processing has long been a crucial aspect of various medical disciplines, including cardiology, audiology, and neurology. However, traditional methods often rely on manual analysis, which is both time-consuming and prone to human error. Moreover, the sheer volume of audio data generated by patients and equipment can overwhelm healthcare systems, leading to delays in diagnosis and treatment.

Moreover, the complexities involved in audio signal processing pose a significant challenge for healthcare professionals. These include:

* Noise reduction and filtering
* Signal denoising and normalization
* Feature extraction and pattern recognition

To address these challenges, we must develop innovative solutions that can efficiently process large datasets and identify meaningful patterns within them.
