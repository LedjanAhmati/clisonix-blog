**The Problem**

EEG data is uniquely vulnerable to unauthorized access and misuse. These records capture an individual's brain activity in real-time, revealing intimate details about their cognitive state. In the wrong hands, this information could be exploited for identity theft, social engineering attacks, or even used to develop targeted phishing campaigns. Moreover, compromised EEG data can also lead to biased AI decision-making, perpetuating existing health disparities.

Clisonix's own Signal Fabric, which integrates EEG, audio, and biosensor streams into a cohesive dataset, further amplifies the risks associated with storing sensitive patient information. When combined with Tide Engine's distributed architecture, which ensures consistent state across multiple nodes, the potential for data breaches is substantial.
