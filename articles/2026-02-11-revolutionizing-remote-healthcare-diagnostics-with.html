**The Problem: Challenges in Edge Computing**

Edge computing's promise of real-time processing and lower latency has not been fully realized in healthcare due to several challenges:

*   **Data Volume and Variety**: Healthcare data is generated from diverse sources (EEG, audio, biosensors) at varying rates, making data management and analysis complex.
*   **Processing Power and Bandwidth**: Remote medical settings often have limited processing power and bandwidth, hindering the adoption of AI-driven analytics.
*   **Security and Compliance**: Medical data requires strict security measures to ensure patient confidentiality and regulatory compliance.
