As we navigate the complexities of modern healthcare, one pressing issue has become increasingly apparent: the need for real-time data analysis. The increasing volume and velocity of medical data require innovative solutions that can process and interpret this information in real-time. In the field of electroencephalography (EEG), edge computing is poised to revolutionize the way we analyze brain activity, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions at the point of care.

**The Problem**

Traditional EEG analysis relies on centralized data processing, which introduces latency and hinders timely decision-making. Data must be transmitted to a remote server for analysis, only to be returned hours or even days later. This approach is not only inefficient but also compromises the accuracy of the results due to data degradation during transmission.

Furthermore, existing edge computing solutions often lack the necessary scalability and adaptability to handle the demands of medical applications. They may require significant infrastructure investments, which can be cost-prohibitive for healthcare organizations.

**Technical Deep Dive**

To address these challenges, our team at Clisonix has developed a cutting-edge architecture that leverages the power of edge computing to analyze EEG data in real-time. Our solution is built around two key components:

1. **Tide Engine**: A distributed healthcare node management system that ensures consistent state across all nodes, even in the presence of network partitions or failures.
2. **Signal Fabric**: A data processing framework that weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams into a unified, time-synchronized dataset.
