**The Problem: Real Challenges in Regulatory Compliance**

Developers and clinicians face numerous challenges when it comes to regulatory compliance for AI-powered EEG devices:

1.  **Inadequate documentation**: Failure to provide detailed technical documentation of the device's architecture, algorithms, and implementation can lead to FDA scrutiny.
2.  **Lack of clinical validation**: Insufficient clinical trials and validation studies can raise concerns about the device's efficacy and safety.
3.  **Inadequate cybersecurity measures**: Failing to implement robust security protocols can compromise patient data and create regulatory risks.
