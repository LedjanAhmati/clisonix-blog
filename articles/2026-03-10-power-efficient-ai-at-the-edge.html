**The Problem: Real Challenges in Edge Computing**

Current edge computing solutions face significant challenges:

1.  **Power Consumption**: Most existing solutions rely on resource-intensive processing, leading to high power consumption and heat generation.
2.  **Data Transfer Latency**: Transferring large amounts of data between devices and cloud-based services introduces unacceptable latency, compromising real-time decision-making capabilities.
3.  **Scalability**: As the number of edge devices grows, maintaining scalability becomes increasingly difficult.

**Technical Deep Dive: Architecture, Algorithms, Implementation**

Clisonix addresses these challenges through a novel approach to power-efficient AI at the edge. Our solution leverages cutting-edge technologies like the Tide Engine and Signal Fabric:

1.  **Tide Engine**: This innovative technology ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes by employing advanced synchronization algorithms.
2.  **Signal Fabric**: By weaving together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams, Signal Fabric provides a unified framework for processing diverse data modalities.

Our architecture is designed to optimize power efficiency through:

1.  **In-Situ Processing**: Real-time processing occurs on the device itself, eliminating unnecessary data transfer.
2.  **Distributed Training**: The Tide Engine enables distributed training across multiple devices, reducing computational overhead and energy consumption.
3.  **Model Pruning**: Our system employs model pruning techniques to minimize memory usage and power consumption.
