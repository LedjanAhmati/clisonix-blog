**The Problem**
Data privacy in healthcare is a multifaceted issue. On one hand, patients expect medical devices to provide personalized care while minimizing risk exposure. On the other hand, regulatory bodies demand strict adherence to data protection standards. Current approaches often fall short due to inadequate data encryption, insufficient user authentication, and lack of transparency about how data is used.

**Technical Deep Dive**
At Clisonix, we're addressing these challenges through our Tide Engine and Signal Fabric technologies. The Tide Engine ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes by implementing a decentralized architecture that leverages blockchain principles. This allows for secure data sharing while maintaining control over sensitive information.

Our Signal Fabric technology weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to create a comprehensive view of patient health. By processing these diverse signals in real-time, our AI-powered medical devices can detect subtle changes in patient status, enabling timely interventions.
