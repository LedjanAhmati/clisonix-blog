**The Problem: Real Challenges in Regulatory Compliance**

Regulatory agencies like the FDA require meticulous documentation and validation of AI-powered medical devices to ensure their safety and efficacy. This includes demonstrating that the device's performance is consistent across different environments, user interactions, and data inputs. In the case of EEG devices, this means proving that the algorithm accurately detects brain activity patterns while minimizing false positives or negatives.

The lack of standardization in AI development frameworks and methodologies further complicates the regulatory compliance process. Clisonix's Tide Engine, a critical component of our architecture, helps address these challenges by ensuring consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes. This guarantees that our AI-powered EEG devices operate cohesively and efficiently, meeting the stringent requirements set forth by the FDA.
