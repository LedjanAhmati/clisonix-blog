Real-time EEG data processing requires high-speed computation and efficient storage, making it a prime target for cyber threats. Moreover, the complex nature of brain signals makes them difficult to anonymize, rendering traditional de-identification techniques ineffective. A single data breach can compromise not only patient confidentiality but also undermine trust in healthcare providers.
