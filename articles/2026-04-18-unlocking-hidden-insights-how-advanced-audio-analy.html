Audio processing in healthcare has traditionally been plagued by challenges such as noise cancellation, speaker identification, and speech recognition. These issues often lead to incomplete or inaccurate data, resulting in poor patient outcomes. Moreover, the sheer volume of audio data generated daily makes manual analysis impractical and time-consuming. To overcome these obstacles, we need innovative solutions that can efficiently process and extract meaningful insights from audio streams.
