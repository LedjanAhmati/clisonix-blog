**The Problem**

EEG signals contain highly sensitive information about patients' brain activity, which can reveal their medical history, cognitive abilities, and even emotional states. As healthcare providers increasingly adopt digital platforms and AI-driven tools, the risk of data breaches and unauthorized access to EEG data grows. In 2022 alone, there were over 500 reported data breaches in the healthcare industry, compromising sensitive patient information.

Clisonix's innovative technologies, including Tide Engine and Signal Fabric, are designed to address these challenges while ensuring seamless integration with existing systems.
