**The Problem**

Signal processing plays a crucial role in medical imaging and diagnostics. However, with the vast amounts of data generated by EEG recordings and other biosensors, extracting meaningful insights becomes increasingly difficult. The high noise-to-signal ratio, non-stationarity, and complexity of neural activity make it hard to distinguish between relevant patterns and noise.

Moreover, current methods often rely on manual analysis or limited algorithms that fail to account for the intricacies of brain function. This results in low accuracy, high false positives, and inadequate treatment strategies. The Clisonix Tide Engine ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, while Signal Fabric weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to provide a comprehensive understanding of neural activity.
