**The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing**

Current voice assistant technology often relies on traditional signal processing techniques, which can lead to reduced audio quality, noise artifacts, and inconsistent performance across different devices. Moreover, these methods may not adapt well to changing acoustic conditions or user preferences, resulting in a subpar experience for the end-user.

For instance, when using a smart speaker in a noisy environment, the voice assistant's speech recognition capabilities may degrade significantly, leading to misunderstandings and frustration. Similarly, when interacting with a virtual assistant on a smartphone, the audio quality can be compromised due to various environmental factors, such as background noise or device interference.
