**The Problem**
Real-world AI and ML systems face numerous challenges that go beyond the confines of traditional IT monitoring. These complex systems rely on intricate architectures, dynamic data flows, and precise control over compute resources to deliver high-quality predictions and recommendations.

* **Model Drift**: As new data streams in, models can deviate from their intended behavior, resulting in suboptimal performance or even failure.
* **Data Quality Issues**: Poorly formatted or inaccurate data can propagate through the system, corrupting results and leading to incorrect decisions.
* **Compute Resource Management**: Managing compute resources efficiently is crucial for ensuring timely model updates, batch processing, and real-time predictions.
