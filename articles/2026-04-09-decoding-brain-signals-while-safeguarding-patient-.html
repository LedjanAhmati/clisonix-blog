**The Problem: Real Challenges in Data Privacy**

EEG signals are highly sensitive and personal, comprising intimate details of an individual's cognitive processes. When processing and analyzing these signals using AI algorithms, there is a high risk of data breaches, unauthorized access, or misuse. Moreover, the increasing adoption of cloud-based services for EEG signal storage and processing exacerbates these concerns.

To address this challenge, we must adopt robust data protection strategies that balance signal fidelity with security. Inadequate measures can lead to compromised patient confidentiality, damage trust in healthcare providers, and undermine the integrity of AI-driven insights.
