**The Problem**

EEG data is particularly vulnerable to unauthorized access due to its high sensitivity and rich spatial-temporal structure. Inadequate encryption strategies can lead to catastrophic consequences, including:

1. **Data breaches**: Unencrypted EEG data can be intercepted or accessed by malicious actors, compromising patient confidentiality.
2. **Unauthorized analysis**: Sensitive information may be extracted from the data without patients' knowledge or consent.
3. **Loss of trust**: Reputational damage and loss of public trust can occur if healthcare providers are perceived as incompetent in protecting sensitive data.

**Technical Deep Dive**

To safeguard EEG data, Clisonix has developed a robust encryption framework that leverages cutting-edge technologies like Tide Engine and Signal Fabric. Our approach involves:

1. **Data preprocessing**: We employ advanced signal processing techniques to normalize and transform the raw EEG signals.
2. **Encryption with homomorphic encryption (HE)**: We use HE-based algorithms, such as those offered by our Signal Fabric platform, to encrypt the preprocessed data without compromising its usability for analytics.
3. **Secure multi-party computation (SMPC)**: Our Tide Engine ensures that sensitive information is only accessible to authorized parties while keeping it confidential.
