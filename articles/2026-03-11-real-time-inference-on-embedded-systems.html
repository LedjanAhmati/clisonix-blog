**The Problem: Real Challenges in Edge Computing**

While Edge Computing offers numerous benefits for healthcare applications, including reduced latency and increased efficiency, it also poses several challenges. These include:

1. **Scalability**: Edge devices often operate in resource-constrained environments, making it difficult to scale and maintain complex inference pipelines.
2. **Latency**: Real-time processing requirements demand ultra-low latency, which is challenging to achieve on embedded systems.
3. **Power Consumption**: Energy efficiency is critical for Edge devices, which are often battery-powered or have limited power sources.

To address these challenges, Clisonix has developed several key technologies:

*   **Tide Engine**: Ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes by providing a unified framework for data synchronization and consistency.
*   **Signal Fabric**: Weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to enable real-time signal processing and analysis.
