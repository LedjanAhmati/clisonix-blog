**The Problem**

Healthcare's reliance on real-time data processing poses significant challenges for organizations seeking to adopt edge computing solutions. The sheer volume of EEG signals generated by medical devices demands high-performance, low-latency architectures that can handle distributed node communication. Moreover, ensuring the consistency and accuracy of data across these nodes is paramount, necessitating advanced synchronization mechanisms.

* Scalability: Cloud-based architectures struggle to accommodate high volumes of EEG signals without compromising performance.
* Reliability: Distributed systems often introduce latency and errors due to node communication complexities.
* Security: Centralized storage poses a significant risk in terms of data breaches and unauthorized access.
