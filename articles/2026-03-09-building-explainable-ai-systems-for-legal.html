**The Problem: Real Challenges in AI ML Systems**

Explainability is a critical aspect of AI system design, especially when dealing with sensitive or high-risk applications. Existing AI approaches often rely on complex models and opaque algorithms, making it challenging to provide clear explanations for decision-making processes. In the context of law enforcement and healthcare, explainability is not just a nicety – it's a necessity.

Consider a scenario where an AI system is tasked with analyzing medical records to identify potential health risks. The system must be able to provide a clear explanation for its recommendations, including the data used, algorithms employed, and decision-making process. Failure to do so can lead to mistrust, regulatory issues, and potentially catastrophic consequences.
