The emergence of brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) has revolutionized the way we interact with technology. In healthcare, BCIs have the potential to transform patient care by enabling more effective and personalized treatments. However, implementing BCIs in an enterprise setting requires a sophisticated framework that addresses scalability, security, and data management.

**The Problem**

Adopting BCIs in healthcare is not without its challenges. The sheer complexity of neural signals, combined with the need for real-time processing, poses significant technical hurdles. Moreover, integrating BCIs with existing healthcare systems requires careful consideration of interoperability, data governance, and regulatory compliance. Furthermore, the lack of standardization in BCI protocols and algorithms adds to the difficulties in adoption.
