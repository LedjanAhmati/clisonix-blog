**The Problem**

Current AI and ML systems suffer from several significant challenges:

1.  **Lack of Transparency**: Black-box models are notoriously difficult to interpret, making it hard for users to understand the reasoning behind predictions.
2.  **Model Drift**: Over time, model performance can degrade due to changes in data distribution or other factors, leading to decreased accuracy and reliability.
3.  **Scalability**: As datasets grow, existing architectures struggle to handle increased computational demands, often compromising latency and throughput.
