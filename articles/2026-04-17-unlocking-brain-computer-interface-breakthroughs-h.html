**The Problem: Real Challenges in Healthcare Tech**

Current BCI systems often face significant hurdles:

1. **Signal Noise**: EEG signals are inherently noisy and sensitive to environmental factors, making it challenging to accurately decode brain activity.
2. **Low Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)**: The SNR is critical for reliable signal processing; however, existing methods struggle to achieve optimal performance.
3. **Limited Scalability**: Most BCIs are designed for specific applications or use cases, hindering their adaptability and broader clinical adoption.
