**The Problem: Real Challenges in Enterprise AI**

While traditional AI solutions have shown promise in various industries, their limitations become apparent when applied to the complex and dynamic environment of healthcare. One major hurdle lies in the sheer volume and velocity of medical data, which demands real-time processing and analysis. Furthermore, the need for robust security, scalability, and reliability adds an extra layer of complexity.

Moreover, existing solutions often rely on centralized architectures, which can lead to:

* Latency issues due to network bottlenecks
* Inefficiencies in data transmission and storage
* Limited ability to adapt to changing requirements

In contrast, edge AI processing offers a more agile and decentralized approach, enabling healthcare organizations to:

* Process data closer to the source, reducing latency
* Enhance data security through local storage and encryption
* Adapt quickly to new medical discoveries and emerging threats
