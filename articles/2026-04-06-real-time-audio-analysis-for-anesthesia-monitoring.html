**The Problem: Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio processing plays a critical role in anesthesia monitoring, as it enables the detection of subtle changes in a patient's physiological state. However, several challenges hinder the effective use of audio analysis techniques:

1. **Signal Quality**: Anesthesia-related sounds can be faint, noisy, or distorted, making signal extraction and filtering a significant challenge.
2. **Sensor-to-Source Variability**: Different anesthesia machines and sensors may have varying signal characteristics, requiring specialized processing algorithms to ensure consistent results.
3. **Real-time Processing**: Fast and accurate decision-making is critical in anesthesia monitoring, necessitating efficient real-time processing capabilities.
