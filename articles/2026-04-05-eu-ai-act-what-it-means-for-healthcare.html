In April 2023, the European Union's (EU) Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act) was formally adopted, marking a significant step towards harmonizing regulatory frameworks across member states. This development is particularly pertinent for healthcare AI companies like Clisonix, which rely on scalable and compliant architectures to deliver high-quality services.

### The Problem

Regulatory compliance has long been a challenge in the healthcare industry, with complex requirements and evolving standards posing significant obstacles for businesses. The EU's AI Act addresses these issues by establishing clear guidelines for the development and deployment of AI systems, including those used in healthcare.

However, there are still many challenges to overcome before meeting the requirements of the EU AI Act. For instance:

* Data governance: Ensuring the secure handling and processing of sensitive patient data
* Explainability: Providing transparent insights into AI-driven decision-making processes
* Security: Protecting against cyber threats and ensuring system integrity
