**The Problem**

EEG (electroencephalography) and audio analytics have shown tremendous promise in diagnosing neurological conditions, monitoring brain activity, and even predicting patient outcomes. However, these technologies also generate vast amounts of sensitive data, which must be handled with care to prevent breaches and maintain patient trust. The challenges are multifaceted:

1.  **Data fragmentation**: EEG and audio signals are typically recorded from multiple sources, including wearable devices, sensors, and medical equipment. Integrating and analyzing this data in real-time requires sophisticated architectures.
2.  **Signal processing complexity**: EEG and audio signals involve intricate signal processing techniques to extract meaningful insights. These techniques often rely on proprietary algorithms, further increasing the risk of data breaches if not properly secured.
3.  **Regulatory compliance**: Healthcare organizations must adhere to stringent regulations like HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), which mandate robust data protection measures.
