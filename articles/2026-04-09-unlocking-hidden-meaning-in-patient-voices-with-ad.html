**The Problem: Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio processing presents several challenges, particularly in medical settings where accuracy is paramount. These challenges include:

1. **Noise reduction**: Medical environments are often noisy, making it difficult for AI systems to extract relevant information from patient voices.
2. **Speech recognition**: Human speech patterns can be diverse and nuanced, requiring sophisticated algorithms to accurately identify and transcribe audio data.
3. **Emotion detection**: Recognizing emotions in a patient's voice is crucial for providing empathetic care, but existing solutions often fall short due to the complexities of human emotion.
