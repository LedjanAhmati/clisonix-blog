### The Problem

Regulatory compliance for medical devices is notoriously complex, especially when it comes to AI-driven systems like those developed by Clisonix. The FDA's guidance on AI, released in 2019, emphasizes the need for transparency, explainability, and validation of these systems. However, the lack of clear standards and regulatory frameworks has led to a situation where manufacturers are often left uncertain about how to navigate compliance.

Moreover, the increasing adoption of AI-powered devices raises concerns about data quality, bias, and security. Ensuring that EEG data is accurate, reliable, and compliant with FDA regulations requires meticulous attention to detail and adherence to best practices.
