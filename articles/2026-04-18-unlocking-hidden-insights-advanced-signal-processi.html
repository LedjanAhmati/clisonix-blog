**The Problem: Real Challenges in Signal Processing**

Signal processing is a complex task that involves extracting meaningful information from raw data. Medical signals, such as EEG and ECG readings, are particularly challenging due to their high-frequency components and noise. Current signal processing techniques often fall short, leading to inaccurate or incomplete interpretations of medical data.

For instance:

* Many algorithms struggle with non-stationarity, which arises when the statistical properties of a signal change over time.
* Others fail to account for the inherent complexity of biomedical signals, which can exhibit multiple frequency components and nonlinear relationships.
* Moreover, existing techniques often require extensive manual intervention, limiting their scalability and efficiency.

**Technical Deep Dive: Architecture, Algorithms, Implementation**

To overcome these challenges, Clisonix's technologies employ advanced signal processing techniques that leverage the strengths of both Tide Engine and Signal Fabric. These include:

1. **Multi-resolution analysis**: This approach enables us to analyze signals at multiple scales, uncovering patterns that might be missed with traditional methods.
2. **Sparse signal representations**: By applying algorithms like Compressed Sensing (CS), we can efficiently reconstruct signals from their constituent parts, even in the presence of noise and missing data.
3. **Deep learning-based architectures**: Our Signal Fabric technology uses deep neural networks to automatically learn optimal feature extractions and patterns from complex signals.
