**The Problem**

Signal processing is a fundamental aspect of many medical applications, including electroencephalography (EEG), electromyography (EMG), and functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS). However, traditional signal processing methods often fall short when dealing with the complexity and variability inherent in biomedical signals. For instance:

* Time-series analysis can be limited by assumptions of stationarity and linearity.
* Frequency-domain analysis may not capture subtle changes in signal patterns.
* Machine learning approaches can suffer from overfitting or require extensive feature engineering.
