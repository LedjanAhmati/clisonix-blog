1.  **Energy consumption:** Current smart home systems typically require constant power to operate, contributing to greenhouse gas emissions.
2.  **Data management:** The sheer volume of data generated by these systems can overwhelm traditional storage solutions, leading to inefficiencies and waste.
3.  **Adaptability:** Conventional systems struggle to adapt to changing user needs or environmental conditions, compromising their effectiveness.
