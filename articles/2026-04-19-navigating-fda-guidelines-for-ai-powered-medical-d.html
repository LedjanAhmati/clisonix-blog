**The Problem: Real Challenges in Regulatory Compliance**

Developing AI-powered medical devices requires compliance with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's (FDA) guidelines, which can be daunting due to their complexity and ever-changing nature. Manufacturers must ensure that their products meet stringent requirements for safety, effectiveness, and performance, while also navigating the nuances of data privacy, cybersecurity, and software validation.

The FDA has introduced regulations such as FDA Guidance on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML) in Medical Devices to help clarify the development process. However, implementing these guidelines can be a significant undertaking, especially for companies with limited experience in regulatory affairs or AI technology.
