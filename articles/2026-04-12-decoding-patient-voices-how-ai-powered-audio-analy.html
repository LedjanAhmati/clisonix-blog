**The Problem: Challenges in Healthcare Tech**

Traditional healthcare IT systems often struggle with data silos, interoperability issues, and a lack of real-time analytics capabilities. These limitations hinder clinicians' ability to provide personalized care, leading to:

1. **Inefficient workflows**: Manual data entry, paper-based records, and outdated electronic health record (EHR) systems slow down clinical workflows.
2. **Insufficient patient engagement**: Static EHRs fail to capture the nuances of a patient's voice, tone, and emotional state – crucial factors in understanding their needs and concerns.
3. **Lack of real-time insights**: Clinicians often rely on delayed feedback from lab results, imaging studies, or patient surveys, making it challenging to respond promptly to changing patient conditions.
