**The Problem**
Real-world AI/ML systems face numerous challenges:

* **Scalability**: As the volume of data grows, traditional processing methods become bottlenecked.
* **Noise and artifacts**: Raw sensor data often contains noise and artifacts that can compromise model accuracy.
* **Concept drift**: Shifts in underlying patterns can cause models to degrade over time.

To address these issues, researchers have turned to wavelet decomposition – a signal processing technique that breaks down signals into their constituent frequency components. By applying wavelet transforms to raw sensor data, AI/ML systems can:

* **Improve feature extraction**: Wavelets capture subtle patterns and anomalies in the data.
* **Enhance noise robustness**: Wavelet-based denoising techniques can filter out irrelevant noise.
