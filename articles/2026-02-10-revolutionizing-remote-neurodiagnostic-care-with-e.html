**The Problem**

Edge computing faces numerous challenges, particularly in healthcare. Data-intensive applications like EEG processing require significant computational resources, which can be difficult to provide in remote settings. Moreover, EEG data is typically transmitted over wireless networks, introducing latency and potential data loss. Current solutions often rely on centralized servers or cloud-based infrastructure, which can lead to delays in test results and compromise patient care.
