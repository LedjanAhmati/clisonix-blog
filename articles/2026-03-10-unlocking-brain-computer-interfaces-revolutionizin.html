**FAQ**

Q: What is the CRDT Merge Layer?
A: The CRDT Merge Layer is a distributed data structure that enables multiple clinicians to edit the same patient data simultaneously, while ensuring data consistency and accuracy.

Q: How does Post-Quantum Encryption work in this context?
A: Post-Quantum Encryption ensures the secure transmission of sensitive medical data between nodes, safeguarding against potential quantum computing threats.

Q: What is the Tide Engine?
A: The Tide Engine is a state management system that guarantees consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, ensuring seamless collaboration and accurate patient data.
