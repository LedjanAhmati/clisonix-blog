The human brain's remarkable ability to adapt and reorganize itself in response to new experiences or environments is known as neuroplasticity. This process underlies our capacity for learning and memory formation throughout life. However, when neuroplasticity falters, cognitive decline can set in, leading to devastating consequences for individuals and society alike. The importance of harnessing EEG signals to decode cognitive reserve has never been more pressing.
