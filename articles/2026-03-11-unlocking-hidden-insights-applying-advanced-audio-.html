**The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio processing has long been a cornerstone of various industries, including music production, speech recognition, and acoustic signal analysis. However, its application in medical diagnostics remains limited due to several challenges:

1. **Signal complexity**: Medical audio signals often exhibit complex patterns, making it difficult for traditional signal processing techniques to extract meaningful information.
2. **Noise sensitivity**: Audio signals are prone to noise corruption, which can lead to inaccurate diagnoses and ineffective treatments.
3. **Interoperability issues**: Integrating audio analytics with existing medical systems poses significant technical hurdles.
