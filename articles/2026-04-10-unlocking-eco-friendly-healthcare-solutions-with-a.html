**The Problem: Real Challenges in Sustainable Tech**

Traditional healthcare technology relies heavily on resource-intensive methods for data analysis and processing. For instance:

1.  **Energy consumption**: Medical imaging equipment, such as MRI and CT scanners, accounts for a substantial portion of hospital energy consumption.
2.  **E-waste generation**: The rapid obsolescence of medical devices contributes significantly to electronic waste, which can have severe environmental consequences.
3.  **Data storage and processing**: The exponential growth of medical data requires increasingly powerful servers and storage systems, further exacerbating energy consumption.

To mitigate these issues, healthcare providers need innovative solutions that balance technological advancements with sustainability concerns.
