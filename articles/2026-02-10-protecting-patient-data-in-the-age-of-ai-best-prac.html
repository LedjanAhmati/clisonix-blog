**The Problem: Real Challenges in Data Privacy**

Healthcare organizations face numerous challenges when it comes to safeguarding patient data:

* **Rising complexity**: The increasing use of AI-powered tools has led to an explosion of sensitive health information, which must be securely stored and processed.
* **Regulatory compliance**: Healthcare providers must adhere to increasingly stringent regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).
* **Cybersecurity threats**: The expanding attack surface created by AI-powered systems has attracted a new breed of sophisticated cyber threats.
