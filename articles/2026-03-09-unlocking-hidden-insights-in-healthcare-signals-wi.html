**The Problem: Challenges in Signal Processing**

Signal processing is a crucial component of healthcare data analysis, enabling clinicians to identify patterns and anomalies in patient data. However, working with EEG and audio signals presents several challenges:

1. **Noise and Interference**: EEG signals are prone to electrical noise from various sources, including the environment and instrumentation.
2. **High-Dimensional Data**: Multimodal datasets (e.g., EEG + audio) generate vast amounts of high-dimensional data, making it difficult to extract meaningful insights.
3. **Variability in Signal Properties**: Signals exhibit varying properties such as amplitude, frequency, and time domain characteristics.

**Technical Deep Dive: Architecture and Implementation**

Clisonix's Tide Engine and Signal Fabric are designed to tackle the challenges mentioned above. The architecture is built around the following key components:

1. **Tide Engine**: This component ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, allowing for real-time data processing and synchronization.
2. **Signal Fabric**: By weaving together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams, Signal Fabric creates a unified framework for multimodal analysis.

The implementation involves the following steps:

1. **Data Ingestion**: Signals are collected from various sources (EEG, audio, biosensors) and fed into the Tide Engine.
2. **Noise Reduction and Preprocessing**: The Signal Fabric applies advanced filtering techniques to remove noise and normalize signal properties.
3. **Feature Extraction**: Relevant features are extracted from the preprocessed signals using algorithms such as wavelet analysis and spectrogram decomposition.
