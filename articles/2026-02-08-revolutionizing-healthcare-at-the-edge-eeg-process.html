**The Problem:**

Edge computing has emerged as a promising solution to address these challenges. By pushing processing and analytics closer to where the data is generated, edge computing reduces latency, improves real-time decision-making, and enables more efficient use of resources. However, existing edge computing solutions often fail to account for the unique requirements of healthcare applications, such as high-precision signal processing, real-time data fusion, and compliance with stringent regulatory standards.
