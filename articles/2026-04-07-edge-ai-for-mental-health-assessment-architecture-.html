**The Problem**

Real-world applications of edge computing face significant challenges. These include:

*   **Scalability**: As datasets grow, traditional architectures often struggle to maintain performance.
*   **Latency**: Real-time processing requirements demand ultra-low latency, making it difficult to balance computational power and energy efficiency.
*   **Security**: Edge devices are often exposed to various threats, requiring robust security measures.

To overcome these challenges, we need innovative architecture patterns that leverage the strengths of edge computing while addressing its limitations.

**Technical Deep Dive**

Our approach involves combining two key technologies:

1.  **Tide Engine**: Ensures consistent state across distributed healthcare nodes, enabling seamless data synchronization and reducing latency.
2.  **Signal Fabric**: Weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams into a cohesive framework for real-time analysis.

These components form the foundation of our edge AI architecture, which we'll outline below:

Our architecture consists of three main layers:

1.  **Data Ingestion**: Signal Fabric collects and preprocesses data from various sources, including EEG, audio, and biosensors.
2.  **Real-Time Analysis**: Tide Engine ensures consistent state across nodes while performing real-time analysis on the preprocessed data.
3.  **Decision Support**: The results of this analysis are fed into a decision support system, which recommends interventions to healthcare professionals.
