**The Problem**

Audio processing has long been a challenging domain due to the complexity and variability of human speech. From EEG signals captured during brain-computer interface (BCI) experiments to biosensor readings from wearable devices, audio data is often contaminated with noise, artifacts, and other sources of interference. Traditional signal processing techniques struggle to extract meaningful information from these noisy streams, resulting in suboptimal outcomes.

To overcome these challenges, healthcare organizations require sophisticated audio analytics solutions that can effectively filter out noise, identify patterns, and predict patient behavior. This is where Clisonix's Signal Fabric comes into play – a novel platform that weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to create a rich tapestry of patient data.
