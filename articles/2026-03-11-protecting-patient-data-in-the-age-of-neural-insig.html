**The Problem**

Real-world challenges in protecting patient data include:

* Ensuring compliance with regulations such as HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
* Preventing data breaches and unauthorized access
* Maintaining the integrity of sensitive information, including medical histories, diagnoses, and treatment plans
* Addressing the complexity of neural network architectures, which can make it difficult to detect and prevent data leaks

To overcome these challenges, healthcare tech developers must adopt a robust approach to data protection that incorporates cutting-edge technologies and best practices.
