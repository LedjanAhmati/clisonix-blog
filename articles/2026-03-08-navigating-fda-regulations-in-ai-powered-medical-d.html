**The Problem: Real Challenges in Regulatory Compliance**

Developing AI-powered medical devices that comply with FDA regulations can be a daunting task. The regulatory landscape is constantly evolving, with new guidelines and updates emerging regularly. Moreover, the complexity of AI algorithms and their interaction with human patients adds an additional layer of scrutiny.

One of the primary challenges lies in understanding the nuances of each regulation. For instance, the 21 CFR Part 11 guideline for electronic records and signatures requires that all data be secure, accurate, and reliable. However, when it comes to AI-powered devices, ensuring the accuracy and reliability of these systems can be a significant challenge.

**Technical Deep Dive: Architecture, Algorithms, Implementation**

Signal Fabric, on the other hand, weaves together EEG, audio, and biosensor streams to provide a comprehensive view of patient data. By leveraging machine learning algorithms, Signal Fabric can identify patterns and anomalies that may indicate potential health issues.
