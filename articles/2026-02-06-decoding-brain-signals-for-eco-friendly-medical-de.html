**The Problem: Electrophysiological Data and Sustainability**

Electrophysiological (EP) data – derived from sources such as EEG, audio, and biosensors – has revolutionized our understanding of neurological functions. However, the sheer volume of EP data generated by modern medical devices poses significant environmental concerns. Traditional approaches to processing and storing this data often rely on resource-intensive infrastructure, resulting in increased carbon emissions and electronic waste.

To tackle these challenges, we need innovative solutions that reconcile signal processing with sustainability goals. Our team has been working tirelessly to develop eco-friendly technologies that meet the demands of precision healthcare while minimizing its ecological footprint.
