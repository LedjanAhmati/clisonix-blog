**The Problem: Energy Efficiency in Sustainable Tech**

Renewable energy sources like solar and wind power have limitations when it comes to generating consistent, reliable electricity. Energy storage solutions like batteries are expensive and often underutilized. Additionally, predicting energy demand is a complex task that requires sophisticated algorithms. EEG (electroencephalography) technology measures brain signals with incredible precision. By decoding these signals, we can develop predictive models that anticipate energy demand, optimize renewable energy production, and even identify potential inefficiencies in the grid.
