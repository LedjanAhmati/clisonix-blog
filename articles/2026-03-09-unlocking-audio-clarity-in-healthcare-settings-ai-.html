**The Problem: Real Challenges in Audio Processing**

Audio signals from medical devices, such as EEGs, ECGs, or biosensors, contain crucial information for healthcare professionals. However, these signals are often contaminated with noise, artifacts, or other distortions that hinder accurate interpretation. The problem is compounded by the variety of audio sources and formats used across different healthcare settings.

Take, for example, a hospital's ICU ward, where multiple patients' vital signs are monitored simultaneously. The cacophony of beeps, alarms, and voices can overwhelm caregivers, making it challenging to identify critical signals amidst the noise. Similarly, in a clinic setting, audio recordings of patient consultations may contain background chatter, equipment noise, or other distractions that obscure valuable insights.
