**Technical Deep Dive**

To grasp the full implications of the EU's AI Act, let's dive into the technical details. The legislation introduces several key concepts:

1. **Explainability**: AI systems must provide clear and transparent explanations for their decisions.
2. **Accountability**: Manufacturers are responsible for ensuring that their AI systems operate in accordance with EU law.
3. **Human Oversight**: Humans must be able to intervene and correct any errors or biases introduced by the AI system.
