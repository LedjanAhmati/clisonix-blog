**The Problem: Real Challenges in Edge Computing**

Edge computing in healthcare faces significant challenges:

1. **Data volume and velocity**: The sheer amount of medical data generated by IoT devices, sensors, and wearables poses a significant processing burden on edge devices.
2. **Latency and real-time processing**: Medical professionals require instant feedback to make informed decisions, making latency a critical concern for edge computing systems.
3. **Security and data protection**: Edge devices often operate in unsecured environments, compromising patient data security.
4. **Data standardization and interoperability**: Different medical devices and systems employ varying data formats, hindering seamless integration.
